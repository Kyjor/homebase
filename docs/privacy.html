Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Homebase (“the App”) is a shared household companion for spending, shopping, and home care. This policy explains what information the App handles.

Account & sync

If you create an account, we store your email, display name, and household data (expenses, budgets, shopping lists, tasks, calendar items, memberships, and related content) using Supabase so you can sign in across devices and collaborate with your household.

Photos

With your permission, you may attach photos to house-care tasks. Those images are uploaded to cloud storage associated with your household so members can view them.

On-device data

Some preferences and cached content may also live on your device so the App works smoothly while you use it.

Purchases

Homebase offers an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription (with a free trial) through the App Store. Payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Third-party services

We use Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage, and Apple for App Store billing. Those services process data as needed to operate the App. We do not sell your personal information.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Do not create an account for a child under 13.

Contact

Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
